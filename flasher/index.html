ESP32 BT Dongle

Turns a classic ESP32 dev board into a dual-mode (Classic + LE) Bluetooth HCI controller over its USB port. Firmware v1.0.0.

1. Flash the firmware

  1. Use a board with the original ESP32 (ESP32-WROOM-32, DevKitC, NodeMCU-32S…). S2/S3/C3/C6 won't work: they have no Classic Bluetooth.
  2. Plug it in by USB and close any program that has its serial port open.
  3. Click Connect, pick the board's port (CP210x, CH340 or CH9102), then Install.
Your browser can't flash boards. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer. Web Serial needs a secure page (https).

If it can't connect, hold the board's BOOT button while you click Connect. After flashing, skip the "Logs & Console" and Wi-Fi options: the dongle has no console.

2. Check the dongle

Sends the dongle's identify command at 921600 baud and shows the reply.

Other ways to flash

Files and checksums are in the latest release. With Python:

python -m esptool --chip esp32 -p COM19 write_flash 0x0 esp32_bt_dongle_v1.0.0_merged.bin

Use your own port: COMx on Windows, /dev/ttyUSB0 on Linux.
